How to run your first 5S red-tag event

LeanPro Academy knowledge library

A red-tag event is the fastest way to start 5S, because it forces one decision about every item in an area: is it needed here, for this work?

Before the event, pick a small, visible area and take baseline photos from fixed spots. Agree a red-tag holding area outside the work zone and name one person who can decide what happens to tagged items.

On the day, the team walks the area together. Any item that is not used regularly gets a red tag with the date, the person tagging it and a reason. Broken items, old drawings and extra stock usually make up most of the tags. Do not argue at the tag stage; just tag it.

Tagged items go to the holding area for a fixed time, often two to four weeks. If nobody asks for an item in that time, the decision owner chooses to scrap it, sell it, return it to stores or move it to where it is really used.

Finish by taking the same photos again and writing down what was freed: floor space, rack space and the number of items removed. Then plan the next step, Set in order, while the area is clear.
