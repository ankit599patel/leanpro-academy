Near-miss reporting: getting people to speak up

LeanPro Academy knowledge library

Near misses are the cheapest lessons in safety: nobody was hurt, yet the same causes could lead to a serious injury next time.

People report near misses when reporting is easy, safe and useful. Make the form short, available on the shop floor and quick to fill in. Accept reports in any language and by photo.

Never punish the person who reports. If reporting brings blame, reports stop and the risk stays hidden.

Respond quickly. Thank the person, review the report within a day or two and tell them what was done. A visible board of reported items and actions shows that reports lead to change.

Count near misses as a positive indicator. A rising number of reports with falling injuries usually means a stronger safety culture, not a more dangerous plant.
