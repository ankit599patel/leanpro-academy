OEE explained: availability, performance and quality

LeanPro Academy knowledge library

OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness) shows how much of the planned time a machine produced good parts at full speed. It multiplies three numbers.

Availability is run time divided by planned production time. Breakdowns, changeovers and waiting for material reduce it.

Performance is the ideal cycle time multiplied by the total count, divided by run time. Minor stops and slow running reduce it.

Quality is good parts divided by total parts. Scrap and rework reduce it.

For example, with 480 minutes planned, 45 minutes of downtime, an ideal cycle of 20 seconds, 1,150 parts made and 1,100 good, availability is about 90.6%, performance about 88.1% and quality about 95.7%. OEE is about 76.4%.

The number itself matters less than the biggest loss behind it. Track the six big losses daily, put them on a simple board and attack the largest one first. Use the OEE calculator in LeanPro Tools to try your own numbers.
