Tiered daily meetings that take 15 minutes, not 60

LeanPro Academy knowledge library

Tiered daily meetings connect the shop floor to plant leadership in a short, predictable rhythm.

Tier 1 is the team at the start of the shift: yesterday’s safety, quality, output and problems, in front of a simple board. Ten minutes, standing up.

Tier 2 brings team leaders and support functions together to take up problems Tier 1 could not solve. Tier 3 is the plant leadership meeting that reviews the most important issues and decides priorities.

Keep each meeting short with a fixed agenda and the same board layout every day. Discuss only exceptions and actions, not every number. Each problem gets an owner and a date, and the next meeting starts by checking those actions.

The meetings work when leaders visit the boards, ask good questions and remove obstacles quickly. When problems raised never get solved, people stop raising them.
