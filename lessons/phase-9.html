Supply Chain, Analytics & AI

Free daily lessons, days 241–270.

Day 241 · Benchmarking

Horizontal Deployment (Yokoten)

Spread a good fix to every similar place.

Horizontal deployment (yokoten) shares a proven improvement with other lines, shifts or sites that have similar processes, so the same problem is prevented everywhere.

Key takeaway: Fix it once, apply it everywhere it fits.

Day 242 · Supply Chain Optimization

Supply Chain Basics

The chain from supplier to customer.

A supply chain covers suppliers, inbound logistics, production, storage, distribution and customers. Its performance depends on material, information and cash flowing smoothly between links.

Key takeaway: The chain is only as strong as its weakest link.

Day 243 · Supply Chain Optimization

Bullwhip Effect

Small changes at the customer, big swings upstream.

The bullwhip effect is the amplification of demand variability as orders move upstream in a supply chain, caused by batching, forecasting on orders rather than consumption, and delays.

Key takeaway: Share real demand to calm the whip.

Day 244 · Supply Chain Optimization

Lead Time Reduction

Shorter supply lead time means less stock and faster response.

Reduce supply chain lead time by shortening order processing, working with nearer or more reliable suppliers, reducing batch sizes and sharing demand information.

Key takeaway: Time is the hidden cost in the chain.

Day 245 · Supply Chain Optimization

Vendor Development

Help suppliers improve, and you improve.

Vendor development works with suppliers to improve quality, delivery and cost through audits, joint problem solving and shared targets.

Key takeaway: Strong suppliers make strong plants.

Day 246 · Supply Chain Optimization

Supply Chain Case Study

An illustrative supply chain improvement.

A supply chain case study connects demand, planning, inventory and supplier performance. This example is illustrative; use your own data for real cases.

Key takeaway: Fix the data and the agreement, then the flow follows.

Day 247 · Supply Chain Optimization

Inventory Blind Spot & End to End Thinking

Look at the whole chain, not one link.

Inventory blind spots include stock in transit, at suppliers or in other locations. End-to-end thinking views the whole flow from supplier to customer so decisions do not just move problems from one place to another.

Key takeaway: Optimize the whole chain.

Day 248 · Supply Chain Optimization

FIFO, FEFO & Reorder Point

Use the oldest first and order on time.

FIFO (first in, first out) uses the oldest stock first. FEFO (first expired, first out) uses stock with the earliest expiry first, which is vital for chemicals and food. The reorder point is average daily use times lead time plus safety stock.

Key takeaway: Right stock, right order, right time.

Day 249 · Advanced Root Cause Analytics

Statistical Root Cause

Use data to prove which factor causes the problem.

Statistical root cause analysis uses tools such as stratification, hypothesis tests and regression to show which inputs significantly affect the output, instead of relying on opinion.

Key takeaway: Let the data name the cause.

Day 250 · Advanced Root Cause Analytics

Regression Basics

Model how an output changes with an input.

Regression fits a line or curve to show the relationship between an input, such as temperature, and an output, such as viscosity. It helps predict results and set the right input level.

Key takeaway: Model the relationship, then control it.

Day 251 · Advanced Root Cause Analytics

Correlation vs Causation

Two things moving together does not prove one causes the other.

Correlation shows that two variables change together. Causation means one actually changes the other. Confirm causation with process knowledge and controlled tests.

Key takeaway: Correlation is a clue, not proof.

Day 252 · Advanced Root Cause Analytics

Data Interpretation

Read data in context before deciding.

Good data interpretation checks how data was collected, looks at variation and trends, compares with targets and considers what else changed at the same time.

Key takeaway: Context turns data into information.

Day 253 · Advanced Root Cause Analytics

Root Cause Analytics Case

Analysis of batch data shows that viscosity correlates with raw material moisture. A controlled test confirms it, and a moisture specification at receipt reduces out-of-spec batches.

Key takeaway: Data plus testing finds the real cause.

Day 254 · Advanced Root Cause Analytics

Assumption Trap & Analytical Strategy

Assumptions feel like facts until tested.

The assumption trap is acting on what "everyone knows" without data. An analytical strategy lists assumptions, tests the important ones and updates decisions when the data disagrees.

Key takeaway: Test what everyone believes.

Day 255 · Advanced Root Cause Analytics

A3 Problem Solving (with Example)

Tell the whole problem-solving story on one page.

An A3 report covers background, current condition, goal, root cause analysis, countermeasures, plan and follow-up on one sheet. It structures thinking and makes the story easy to share and review.

Key takeaway: One page, one problem, clear thinking.

Day 256 · AI in Manufacturing

Predictive Maintenance

Fix it just before it would fail.

Predictive maintenance uses condition data, such as vibration, temperature and current, to predict when a component will fail. AI models can find patterns in this data and give earlier warnings.

Key takeaway: Predict the failure, plan the repair.

Day 257 · AI in Manufacturing

AI Quality Inspection

Cameras and models that check every unit.

AI quality inspection uses cameras and trained models to detect defects such as wrong labels, missing caps or damaged cartons. It inspects every unit at line speed but needs good images and regular checking.

Key takeaway: Inspect every unit, verify the model.

Day 258 · AI in Manufacturing

Data Modeling

Turn process data into useful predictions.

Data modeling builds mathematical or AI models from historical data to predict outcomes such as yield or failure. Models are only as good as the data and must be validated before use.

Key takeaway: Good data makes good models.

Day 259 · AI in Manufacturing

Automation Risk

Automating a bad process makes bad results faster.

Automation risks include high cost, loss of flexibility, new failure modes and skill loss. Simplify and stabilize the process before automating it.

Key takeaway: Simplify before you automate.

Day 260 · AI in Manufacturing

AI in Manufacturing Case

A plant uses camera inspection on its labeling line. Wrong and skewed labels are rejected automatically, and the data shows which shift and roll cause most issues, guiding root cause work.

Key takeaway: AI finds defects; people fix causes.

Day 261 · AI in Manufacturing

Blind Technology Adoption & Future Ready Leadership

Technology should solve a real problem.

Blind technology adoption buys tools because they are new, not because they are needed. Future-ready leaders start from the problem, pilot small, build skills and scale what works.

Key takeaway: Problem first, technology second.

Day 262 · AI in Manufacturing

Maintenance Planning & Scheduling

Plan the job, then schedule the time.

Maintenance planning defines what work is needed, with parts, tools, skills and steps. Scheduling assigns the planned work to a time slot agreed with production, so jobs are done efficiently and on time.

Key takeaway: Planned work is faster and safer work.

Day 263 · Advanced OEE Governance

OEE Maturity

OEE matures from measurement to management.

OEE maturity grows from manual tracking, to accurate loss data, to daily action on losses and finally to OEE used in planning and investment decisions.

Key takeaway: Measure, act, then manage by OEE.

Day 264 · Advanced OEE Governance

Loss Tree

Break total loss into its causes.

A loss tree starts from total planned time and splits it into availability, performance and quality losses, then into specific causes. It shows clearly where time is lost.

Key takeaway: See every loss on one tree.

Day 265 · Advanced OEE Governance

Cross-Functional Review & Daily OEE Meeting

Review losses together, every day.

A daily OEE meeting brings production, maintenance and quality together for a short review of yesterday's OEE, top losses and actions. Cross-functional review ensures problems are solved, not passed on.

Key takeaway: Short daily reviews, shared ownership.

Day 266 · Advanced OEE Governance

OEE Governance Case

A plant standardizes OEE definitions across lines and starts a daily review. The real OEE is lower than reported, but losses are now clear, and focused actions raise it steadily.

Key takeaway: True numbers lead to real improvement.

Day 267 · Advanced OEE Governance

Manipulated Reporting & Transparent Performance Culture

Honest data builds a strong culture.

Manipulated reporting hides losses to look good. A transparent performance culture welcomes bad news early, focuses on solving problems and never punishes honest reporting.

Key takeaway: Bad news early is good news.

Day 268 · Advanced OEE Governance

Daily Management System & Escalation

A routine that manages the plant every day.

A daily management system links tier meetings, visual boards, standard work for leaders and an escalation process. Problems that cannot be solved at one level move quickly to the next level with clear time limits.

Key takeaway: Escalate fast, solve at the right level.

Day 269 · Strategic Cost Reduction

Cost Drivers

Find what really drives your costs.

Cost drivers are the factors that cause cost, such as batch size, changeovers, scrap, energy use and transport. Reducing the driver reduces the cost permanently.

Key takeaway: Manage the driver, not the number.

Day 270 · Strategic Cost Reduction

Value Engineering

Keep the function, remove the cost.

Value engineering studies each part of a product or process to keep its function while reducing cost, for example by simplifying packaging or using standard materials.

Key takeaway: Same function, lower cost.
